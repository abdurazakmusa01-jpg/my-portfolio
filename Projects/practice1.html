2. Text formatting (inline elements)

strong: very important text (usually bold)

em: emphasised text (usually italic)

b: bold only for looks, with no extra meaning

i: italic only for looks, such as a foreign word

mark: highlighted text, like a marker pen

small: side comments and fine print

del:removed text

ins:inserted text

u: underlined text (use rarely: links are also underlined)
